Know before you go

What to wear

Light, comfortable clothes, closed shoes, sunglasses and a scarf for the sun and sand.

What to bring

Sunscreen, a camera and a warm layer for the evening. Leave valuables at your hotel.

Best season

October to April is mild. In summer, morning and evening tours are the most comfortable.

Health and safety

Dune bashing is not recommended for pregnant guests or anyone with serious back problems.
